06Questions

Straight answers.

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization is the work of making a website easy for AI search engines to read, trust and quote. It covers whether AI crawlers are allowed in, whether the page content is visible without JavaScript, whether facts are written as short self-contained passages, whether structured data describes the business, and whether the brand is mentioned on the sources AI engines draw from.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims for a high position in a list of links. GEO aims to be one of the few sources an AI engine quotes inside its answer. The foundations overlap: a fast, crawlable, well-structured site helps both. GEO adds work on citable passages, AI crawler access, entity and brand signals, and machine-readable summaries such as llms.txt.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will cite my business?

No. Nobody controls what an AI engine chooses to cite, and anyone who promises it is guessing. What we can do is remove the known blockers, make your content easier to quote, and track before and after which engines mention you for the questions your customers ask.

How long does an audit take?

The free snapshot is a one-page summary sent within two working days. The full audit with fixes usually takes one to two weeks, depending on the size of the site and how quickly we get access to it.

What do you need from me to start?

For the snapshot, only your website address. For the fixes, we need a way to change the site: an editor login for your website platform, or a developer on your side who can apply the changes we hand over.
