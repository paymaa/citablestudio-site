01Engines

Five AI answers, each reading your site differently.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT search links to the pages it draws on. OpenAI says OAI-SearchBot is the crawler that decides whether a site can appear in those results. GPTBot is a separate crawler used for model training, and you can allow one without the other.

Crawlers involved
OAI-SearchBot (search)ChatGPT-User (a page a user asks about)GPTBot (training, optional)
What I check for it
Whether the search crawler is allowed, whether your content is in the page without JavaScript, and whether key facts stand on their own when quoted.

Claude

Claude can search the web and open pages when a question needs current information, and it cites the pages it used. Anthropic publishes three crawlers: one for search, one for pages a user asks about, and one for training.

Crawlers involved
Claude-SearchBot (search)Claude-User (a page a user asks about)ClaudeBot (training, optional)
What I check for it
Crawler access, plainly stated facts with dates and named authors, and pages that read correctly without scripts.

Perplexity

Perplexity shows numbered sources beside its answers, so a page it uses gets a visible link. PerplexityBot builds the index it searches. Perplexity-User fetches a page when someone asks about it.

Crawlers involved
PerplexityBotPerplexity-User
What I check for it
Crawler access, a direct answer near the top of each key page, and visible publication or update dates.

Gemini

Gemini can ground its answers in Google Search results. Google-Extended is a separate robots.txt token that controls whether your content is used for Gemini. Google says it does not affect your ranking in Search.

Crawlers involved
GooglebotGoogle-Extended
What I check for it
Google indexing health, your Google-Extended setting, and structured data that matches what is on the page.

AI Overviews

Google says a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet in Search to appear as a link in AI Overviews, and that no special markup is required. Pages that block indexing or snippets are left out.

Crawlers involved
Googlebot
What I check for it
Indexing, snippet settings such as nosnippet, page speed, and whether the visible text answers the question directly.

Each engine reaches your site through its own crawlers and its own rules. A page one of them can quote may be closed to another.

08Questions

Straight answers.

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the work of making a website easy for AI search engines to read, trust and quote. It covers five things: whether AI crawlers are allowed in, whether the content is in the page without JavaScript, whether facts are written as short self-contained passages, whether structured data describes the business accurately, and whether the business is described consistently on other sites. The term is new and the practice is still settling, so treat any precise formula with suspicion.

How is GEO different from SEO?

GEO and SEO share most of their foundations but aim at different results. SEO aims for a high position in a list of links. GEO aims for your page to be one of the sources an AI engine quotes or links inside its answer. A fast, crawlable, well-structured site helps both. GEO adds attention to AI crawler access, passages that make sense when quoted alone, and consistent facts about the business across the web.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will cite my business?

No, nobody can guarantee that ChatGPT, Google or any other AI engine will cite your business. The engines choose their own sources, and their answers change from one run to the next. What Citable Studio can do is remove the blockers that are known and documented, make your pages easier to quote, and record before and after which engines mention you for an agreed list of customer questions. You get a measurable change to your own site, not a promise about someone else's product.

Do I have to let AI companies train on my content to be cited?

No, being cited in AI search does not require letting AI companies train on your content. OpenAI and Anthropic each run separate crawlers for search and for training, so robots.txt can allow OAI-SearchBot and Claude-SearchBot while blocking GPTBot and ClaudeBot. Google works in a similar way: Googlebot covers Search and AI Overviews, while the Google-Extended token covers Gemini. The audit shows which crawlers your site currently allows, and you decide which ones stay open.

The timeline depends mostly on the size of the site and on how quickly that access arrives.
